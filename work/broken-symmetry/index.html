Broken Symmetry

Vanson Leathers × Blackpicasso · Fashion film · Paris · SS27

Arriving in Paris without asking permission. The city offers perfect symmetry; the jackets break it. The collection takes the room look by look, and the designer appears only at the end — in colour, while the audience turns black and white.
